Why now

Agents don't just find vendors anymore. They shortlist them.

The first round of vendor research is moving to AI: who does this, what does it cost, will it meet our requirements, are they a fit for a company like ours, and what's the next step. Whatever the agent couldn't find, understand, or use isn't part of the answer your buyer reads.

🔎

Being mentioned isn't the same as being chosen

Showing up in an AI answer is step one. The buyer's agent still has to understand your offer, check your pricing and fit, and compare you with alternatives. Most companies only measure the first step.

🕳️

The losses are silent

An agent that can't read your pricing, run your estimator, or find your quote path doesn't send an error. It moves on to a vendor it can understand. No bounce, no lost-deal reason, no signal.

🏁

It's a race, and it's relative

The agent isn't asking whether you're good enough. It's asking who is easiest to understand and act on. Every competitor that gets there first makes you harder to choose.

60-second self-check

Could a buyer's agent get through your customer journey?

Six questions, one for each stage we measure. Answer honestly. "Not sure" is the most common answer, and the most expensive one.

01 · Discover

Can an agent reach and find your key pages?

Without being blocked by bot protection, and with signals that point it to the right pages.

02 · Understand

Would it describe what you do the way you would?

Your offer, who it's for, and the problems you solve, stated clearly and consistently.

03 · Evaluate

Can it retrieve the details buyers check?

Pricing or how pricing works, capabilities, specifications, integrations, certifications, and documentation.

04 · Compare

Would it represent you fairly against alternatives?

Comparable facts and clear differentiators, so you're described on your own terms when a buyer asks "who else?"

05 · Qualify

Could it tell whether you're a fit?

Who you serve, company size, industries, requirements, and proof points it can use to judge fit.

06 · Act

Could it take the next step for the buyer?

Request a quote, book a demo, or run your calculator or configurator, not just read about it.

What is the AgentSee Assessment?

The Assessment is a free, automated review of how well your public website works for AI agents. It runs around 100 checks across six dimensions and returns an absolute 0-100 score, an A-F grade, dimension scores, the most important findings, and anonymous context on how comparable companies score. It shows what is working and what is missing. The detailed fixes come in the paid stages.

Why is the Assessment free, and what's the catch?

There is no catch. The fastest way to show whether this matters for your business is to measure it, and the Assessment is automated, so it costs us little to run. It is useful on its own: you can act on the findings yourself. If you want to go deeper, the Audit and the stages after it are paid. There is no obligation to talk to us.

Who is AgentSee for?

Mid-size B2B companies whose buyers research and compare carefully before they talk to sales: manufacturers, distributors, industrial and professional services firms, and software companies. If winning a deal depends on a buyer understanding your capabilities, specifications, pricing, and fit, an AI agent may already be doing that research first.

Is this just GEO or AEO?

No. Getting found and cited in AI answers matters, but it is only the first step. A buyer's agent also has to understand what you do, evaluate pricing and fit, compare you with alternatives, and take a next step. AEO, GEO, structured data, APIs, and MCP are all tools we use. The goal is a customer journey that works end to end.

How do you measure without access to our systems?

The Assessment looks only at what is publicly available, the same way an outside agent would. Our crawler identifies itself honestly and respects robots.txt. We never bypass bot protection, so if your site blocks automated agents, that shows up as a finding rather than being worked around.

What does "Agent Executable" mean?

It means an automated agent can technically get through a workflow on your site: reach it, provide inputs, run it, and retrieve the result. A calculator, configurator, or quote form that works for people but that an agent cannot operate is present, but not executable. Whether a reasoning agent can use it well is a deeper question we answer in the Audit.

Does the score change when my competitors change?

No. The score and grade have a stable, absolute meaning. Competitor and category benchmarks are shown separately as context, with the number of companies compared, so a benchmark never quietly redefines your grade.

What about MCP and WebMCP?

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) and WebMCP let agents call a business's capabilities directly instead of reading pages and guessing. They matter at the action end of the journey. We recommend and implement them when they fit your buyers and your business, not by default.
