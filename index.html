What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO is the practice of structuring content so AI answer engines — like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews — can accurately extract, quote, and cite it. Where SEO optimizes for a ranked list of links, AEO optimizes for being the source an agent pulls from to construct its answer.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the discipline of earning visibility and citation inside AI-generated answers themselves, not just in an index of links. It focuses on the content structure, authority signals, and phrasing that generative models are more likely to reference and recommend.

What is the Model Context Protocol (MCP)?

MCP is an open standard that lets AI agents connect to external tools, data, and services through a consistent interface. Exposing business capabilities via MCP means an agent can check pricing, availability, or complete an action directly, instead of just reading a page and guessing.

What is WebMCP?

WebMCP brings agent-native interaction into the browser itself, letting an AI agent act within a website's own interface the way a human would — instead of needing a separate API integration for every task.

Why does my website need to be ready for AI agents?

Because a growing share of discovery and research now happens through AI agents acting on a person's behalf. If an agent can't find clean answers or structured data on a site, it recommends a competitor instead — quietly, with no error message to alert you.
