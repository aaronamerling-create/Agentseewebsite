Why now

Agents don't just find vendors anymore. They shortlist them.

The first round of vendor research is moving to AI: who does this, what does it cost, will it meet our requirements, are they a fit for a company like ours, and what's the next step. Whatever the agent couldn't find, understand, or use isn't part of the answer your buyer reads.

🔎

Being mentioned isn't the same as being chosen

Showing up in an AI answer is step one. The buyer's agent still has to understand your offer, check your pricing and fit, and compare you with alternatives. Most companies only measure the first step.

🕳️

The losses are silent

An agent that can't read your pricing, run your estimator, or find your quote path doesn't send an error. It moves on to a vendor it can understand. No bounce, no lost-deal reason, no signal.

🏁

It's a race, and it's relative

The agent isn't asking whether you're good enough. It's asking who is easiest to understand and act on. Every competitor that gets there first makes you harder to choose.

What does AgentSee do?

AgentSee helps mid-size B2B companies win buyers who research with AI agents. We show what actually happens when an agent researches, compares, qualifies, and tries to act on your business (Audit), agree what to change and in what order (Architect), do the work (Activate), and keep watching agents, competitors, and results (Adapt). The free Assessment is the starting point.

What happens in an Audit?

Real AI agents research, compare, qualify, and try to act on your business, the way your buyers' agents would. We test your tools and workflows and benchmark you against the competitors you name. You get evidence of what agents got right, what they got wrong, where they got stuck, and how that compares with your competitors, plus why it happened.

How is the Audit different from the free Assessment?

The Assessment is automated and checks whether your public site gives agents what they need. The Audit shows what reasoning agents actually do with it, in realistic buying tasks, side by side with your competitors. The Assessment tells you what is wrong or missing. The Audit tells you why it matters and where it costs you.

Do you do the implementation work, or just recommend it?

Both. Architect turns the evidence into a prioritized plan built around your goals and what you will permit. Activate does the work: content, structured data, tools, and machine interfaces such as APIs and MCP where they fit. Every change gets the right level of review, and you approve consequential changes before they go live.

Do we have to buy every stage?

No. Engagements usually start with an Audit, because the plan depends on the evidence. After that you decide stage by stage. Adapt is ongoing, for companies that want continuous monitoring once changes have shipped.

What does Adapt include?

Ongoing monitoring of how agents see and use your business, how your competitors change, and what results the work produced. We re-measure regularly and give you a clear call: stay the course, test something, make tactical changes, or start new work. Larger new work is scoped separately through Architect and Activate.

How much do the paid services cost?

Each engagement is scoped to your site, your competitors, and your goals, so we quote after a short conversation. Your free Assessment gives us both a head start.

How much time does it take from our team?

The Audit works from public information, so it needs little more than a conversation about your goals and competitors. Architect needs your input on priorities and constraints. Activate needs access to the systems we change and your approval where changes are consequential.

What is the AgentSee Assessment?

The Assessment is a free, automated review of how well your public website works for AI agents. It runs over 100 checks across six dimensions and returns an absolute 0-100 score, an A-F grade, dimension scores, the most important findings, and anonymous context on how comparable companies score. It shows what is working and what is missing. The detailed fixes come in the paid stages.

Why is the Assessment free?

The fastest way to show whether this matters for your business is to measure it, and the Assessment is automated, so it costs us little to run. It is useful on its own, and it is the natural starting point if you want to go further with an Audit. There is no obligation to talk to us.

Who is AgentSee for?

Mid-size B2B companies whose buyers research and compare carefully before they talk to sales: manufacturers, distributors, industrial and professional services firms, and software companies. If winning a deal depends on a buyer understanding your capabilities, specifications, pricing, and fit, an AI agent may already be doing that research first.

Is this just GEO or AEO?

No. Getting found and cited in AI answers matters, but it is only the first step. A buyer's agent also has to understand what you do, evaluate pricing and fit, compare you with alternatives, and take a next step. AEO, GEO, structured data, APIs, and MCP are all tools we use. The goal is a customer journey that works end to end.

How do you measure without access to our systems?

The Assessment and the Audit look only at what is publicly available, the same way an outside agent would. Our crawler identifies itself honestly and respects robots.txt. We never bypass bot protection, so if your site blocks automated agents, that shows up as a finding rather than being worked around.

What about MCP and WebMCP?

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) and WebMCP let agents call a business's capabilities directly instead of reading pages and guessing. They matter at the action end of the journey. We recommend and implement them when they fit your buyers and your business, not by default.
