Why now

Agents don't just find vendors anymore. They evaluate them.

Buyers now hand an AI agent the first round of research: who does this, what does it cost, does it work with our stack, is it a fit for a company like ours, and what's the next step. The agent reads your site, your docs, your pricing, and your tools, then reports back. Whatever it couldn't find, understand, or use simply isn't part of the answer.

🔎

Being mentioned isn't the same as being chosen

Showing up in an AI answer is step one. The buyer's agent still has to understand your offer, check your pricing and fit, and compare you with alternatives. Most companies only measure the first step.

🕳️

The gaps are silent

An agent that can't read your pricing, run your calculator, or find your demo path doesn't send an error. It moves on to a vendor it can understand, and you never see the lost evaluation in your analytics.

🏁

Your competitors are being measured too

Agent readiness is relative. The question isn't only whether an agent can work with you. It's whether it can work with you better than with the company you're compared against.

What is the AgentSee Snapshot?

The Snapshot is a free, automated assessment of how well your public website works for AI agents. It runs around 100 checks across six dimensions and returns an absolute 0-100 score, an A-F grade, dimension scores, the most important findings, and anonymous context on how comparable companies score. It tells you what is working and what is missing. The detailed fixes come in the paid stages.

Is this just GEO or AEO?

No. Getting found and cited in AI answers matters, but it is only the first step. A buyer's agent also has to understand what you do, evaluate pricing and fit, compare you with alternatives, and take a next step. AEO, GEO, structured data, APIs, and MCP are all tools we use. The goal is a customer journey that works end to end.

What does "Agent Executable" mean?

It means an automated agent can technically get through a workflow on your site: reach it, provide inputs, run it, and retrieve the result. A calculator that works for people but that an agent cannot operate is present, but not executable. Whether a reasoning agent can use it well is a deeper question we answer in the Audit.

How do you measure without access to our systems?

The Snapshot looks only at what is publicly available, the same way an outside agent would. Our crawler identifies itself honestly and respects robots.txt. We never bypass bot protection, so if your site blocks automated agents, that shows up as a finding rather than being worked around.

Does the score change when my competitors change?

No. The score and grade have a stable, absolute meaning. Competitor and category benchmarks are shown separately as context, with the number of companies compared, so a benchmark never quietly redefines your grade.

Who is AgentSee for?

We start with mid-market B2B software companies, roughly 50 to 300 employees, where buyers research and compare vendors carefully and capabilities, integrations, pricing, and fit decide the deal. That is where AI-assisted buying shows up first.

What about MCP and WebMCP?

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) and WebMCP let agents call a business's capabilities directly instead of reading pages and guessing. They matter at the action end of the journey. We recommend and implement them when they fit your buyers and your product, not by default.
